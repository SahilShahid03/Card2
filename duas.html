Duas & Blessings

May Allah Bless Their Journey

A collection of prayers and heartfelt supplications for Shariq, Eenara, their families, and everyone sharing in this celebration.

بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
✦
اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَهُمَا وَبَارِكْ عَلَيْهِمَا وَاجْمَعْ بَيْنَهُمَا فِي خَيْرٍ

O Allah, bless them, shower Your blessings upon them, and bring them together in goodness.

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.

Qur'anic dua: Surah Al-Furqan 25:74.

For Shariq & Eenara

May Allah fill their lives with love, mercy, understanding and lasting happiness. May their home be a place of sakinah, kindness and barakah.

For Their Marriage

May Allah grant them a marriage rooted in faith, patience, respect and companionship, and guide them toward what is best in every chapter of life.

For Both Families

May Allah bless both families, strengthen the bonds between them, and fill their homes with peace, health, happiness and abundance.

For Everyone Attending

May Allah reward everyone who joins this gathering, accept their duas, protect their loved ones, and return them home with happiness in their hearts.

For This Gathering

May this celebration be filled with goodness and barakah, and may every smile, prayer and kind word become a source of blessing.

For Peace & Mercy

May Allah grant Shariq and Eenara a future home filled with sakinah, mawaddah and rahmah, and keep them close to what pleases Him.

Shariq & Eenara
With love, prayers & blessings from our families.
Pithi · Friday, 30 October 2026 · Karachi
